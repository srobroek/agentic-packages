## IC-Specific Guidance

- Metrics that matter: scale (users, requests, data), business impact ($, %), technical scope (services/teams affected), adoption (teams using your tools)
- Do NOT use management metrics: "managed team of 8", "conducted reviews"
- Show the T-shape: deep in primary domain, broad across adjacent areas
- For framework/tool expertise, show what you BUILT with it, not just that you know it
- Link to GitHub repos or open-source work if meaningful
