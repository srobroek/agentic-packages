## Leadership-Specific Guidance

- Frame influence, not authority: "Led cross-functional team" not "Managed direct reports"
- IC-to-leadership transition language:
  - Tech lead → "Led X-person cross-functional team"
  - Mentored juniors → "Developed talent pipeline: mentored X engineers, Y promoted"
  - Architecture decision → "Set technical strategy for platform serving XM users"
- Key Achievements section goes ABOVE experience — recruiters scan top-down
- Budget/revenue language signals leadership even without "Director" title
- Emphasize stakeholder management, executive communication, strategic planning
